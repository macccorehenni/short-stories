"I'm scared, Emrie."

Curiosity pulled her tail tighter around her teifling girlfriend's arm as they flew through the morning air towards the cat girl's former home. She shivered in the ocean air, thick with mist, despite her thick winter jacket and scarf. She had been in flight since she was ten— thirteen years ago, over a decade ago. Yet, she still could not shake the foreboding feeling— what if her bullies returned?  Seventeen years before, her mother— whose name Curiosity long since had forgotten— had abandoned her, causing her to become an orphan, her father long before that abandoning them… Curiosity. Unusually, too, at around the time of her abandonment, she had her first Fey metamorphosis at six, gaining the power to glow. She quite liked this, as she could sneak around without needing a light source. Yet, this was when personalities were shown, and being a part of the group of Secoverite street children, these personalities are not kind. Due to her difference, she was not welcome.

She was pushed around, attacked just beacuse, and hurt for what little money she could scrounge up. She hid from these street urchins, but they always found her. Add to this the Portal Police always telling her she needed to move— move where the _evil_ children lived. After two years of living in fear, the Police finally had enough of Curiosity. Perhaps she was different enough for the police to be _scared_ of her, perhaps it was the street urchins tattling on her for _living_, or perhaps bribing to get her in jail. Whatever the reason, she ended up in jail, her eyes slowly darkening, as if pushing _more_ light to make her seen. At ten, she had had enough. She remembered the Soren sisters, who at that point had just killed their nemisis… again. She decided— she would be like them. She would no longer be abused. So she fled.

She fled fast, running away, using her cat–like reflexes to avoid her villans. But once she left the influence of the Secoverite street scene, she wondered… where next? She then remembered her influences— Ammareth and Givly Soren— and ran towards the Approach Valley. But she initially went the wrong way— towards the only part of Light Mythra that is still fraught with Shadows. Fortunately, she had encountered what would be not okay for most— hitchhiking. Of course, it did look suspicious. As a six–year–old who was alone, no parents, she was lucky enough to find someone who was willing to put ethics aside to ferry her. She knew not which way she was meant to go— obviously— but luckily her destination wasn't too far off. With the help of a few people, she was able to make her way to College City. Yet— she got cold feet. She worried she wouldn't fit in. She hovered around the fruits of her idols' suffering, continuing to suffer herself. Every time she tried to approch them, she faltered. She thought, "I'd _never_ fit in." Over and over, when she tried to approch the Soren sisters, she'd stumble, doubting herself. But something felt different about College City, and as she grew into a young teen, she realized something— they _cared_ about others, unlike those bullies. Both had since become goddesses in their own rights, but that all but made Curiosity falter and fail to enroll in the College as she had wanted to. She faltered, time after time, to find her footing, through her teenaged years. She had found a home, but she still felt like a shadow when compared to the Soren sisters and their friends. 

"You'll be okay, glowcat." Emrie replied, pulling in Curiosity as much as she could while still safely guiding Casorie to a landing, which was a lot more than a less–trained teifling would have been able to. "I _completely_ understand your fear. I'll be here when you're ready— and so will Casorie."

The red duskscale dragon, one who Emrie had known since she was a hatchling, gently nuzzled Curiosity's neck, something which had succeeded at calming the somewhat skittish cat before. But a visible tension remained in her— her tail looped around her girlfriend, her ears flat against the top of her hair, still shivering. 

Emrie pulled her girl closer to her. "Look at me, Curiosity. This _will_ be difficult, girl. But you have me, a spiky friend, and a protective _red_ dragon— never mind her soft fluffy feathers—" she smiled at Casorie, who grunted back in like, as her handler continued— "You will not get hurt. You will _not_." 

"What if… _they_… come back?" By this point the normally goofy cat had tears running down her moon–grey skin, glowing less than usual today. 


"If they do, Quill, Casorie, and I _will_ be ready to fight back. Right, you two?"

Casorie pushed her muzzle deeper into Curiosity's long hair, as Quill scampered up her shoulder to his normal vantage point, nuzzling her as well. 

"Quill, can I join you, spiky man?"

Quill allowed Emrie to place her head on the cat's shoulder as Curiosity slumped to meet her.

"Take your time, glowcat. We'll be here."
