# Chapter 5– The Walking Flight
## Irin
I could feel the tension bleeding out of the three of us the farther we got from the Grandfather Tree village. Like someone finally loosened a too‑tight braid. Everything felt… quieter. Softer. And Yae — gods, she felt like herself again. Patient with my nonsense, but actually present enough to care. I didn’t realize how much I’d missed that until it came back.

“So… Ferris, where to next?” I asked, mostly because the spark between him and my sister was practically humming, and someone had to acknowledge it before it set the forest on fire.

He glanced over, all composed ranger calm. “Perhaps we shall go visit my friends, the ents.”

Ents.
A word I’d never heard before. Living trees — ancient and young at the same time, wise enough to make you feel like a toddler, patient enough not to say it out loud. I’d never seen one, obviously. I’d barely been outside the Malynor castle town before all this. But after seeing the Grandfather Tree guards — those towering, bark‑skinned giants who looked like they’d stepped out of a bedtime story — I could imagine it.
 
And the Tree itself… well, that was supposed to be a tree‑ent who gave up its mobility to hold up the High Forest. I only knew that because I’d stolen one of Yae’s books once. *Creatures of the World*. Most of it was boring, but that part stuck with me. A creature choosing to root itself so others could thrive. I didn’t understand it then. I’m not sure I do now.

But walking beside my sister — alive, breathing, laughing again — and Ferris, who somehow understood both of us without trying…
I think I was starting to.

"We're here."
I gingerly tested my joints as we paused and remarked, "Damn, these long days are starting to take a toll on my knees."
Both Ferris and Yaereene shot looks at me. But I didn't notice. 
"What?" I asked. "I'm allowed to hurt."
I cracked a grin at my sister and her boyfriend.
"In any case, we _are_ here, right, Ferris? So we get to take a break?"
"Maybe, maybe not."

Of course it made me mad. Neither Yaereene nor Imelda has ever managed to get under my skin the way he does. Ferris always had this way of speaking — that cool, measured tone that somehow feels like he’s saying, I’m above you both without ever needing the words. And I let it needle me that day. I let it get right into my ribs.
But then he reached into his jacket and pulled out this tiny lozenge‑shaped candy, like that was the most normal thing in the world. I froze. I actually froze. I told him no — obviously — until he told me what it was. And then everything in me stuttered.


"Here, try this. It's said to heal _any_ physical alilment. Though I really _should not_ trust my mother after what she and my father did to Brell."

I hesitated.
