# The Nap
The nap happens at four. No earlier, no later. Milao went and searched for her mother, but she wasn't anywhere nearby. But she found Veyra, and started nipping at her aunt's ankles. Vey responded using her sound-word for Kessa's daughter, then with a small, raspy cat trill she picked up from Rivlion and Riven that she understood to mean "_I'm coming._" Milao tilted her head, then pattered away, chirping for her sibling and her comforting size. Riven jumped down from the top of the counter, where she had been grabbing a snack. She churped, a long, slow,  airy chirp, and followed her sister, slowly looking for anyone nearby and nudging her sister towards them. Once Milao was certain all of the third generation was gathered, she and Riven headed towards the field as Nivara drew a long breath, a sound-word specific to her sisters telling them she was ready. As she said this, Milao darted behind her ankles, wrapping her long Cat-Talos tail around her sister, giving a Milao–coded hug, with a purr accenting her excited chirps. Nivara grunted and chirred at her sisters on their way to the Rock. They all settled in to their spots on the Rock when they got there:
- Riven first, settling into a comfortable sitting position, their head already drooping, their body shifting into a more masculine protective state;
- Milao settling on their lap, curling up as her sibling's body wrapped around her as it changed, purring, tail wrapped around Nivyra's arm;
- And Nivyra, sitting next to her siblings, her shoulder against Riven's, her arm draped across Milao's back. 
Next to arrive was Veyra, who had found Aurelia, her youngest sibling, yet the leader of the Triad. She grunted softly and lovingly at her sister, slow-blinking at her daughter in her mythic position, then slow-blinked at Lia. Rivlion and Kessa joined next, holding a conversation about their children, specifically Milao. Kessa chirped at her younger daughter, who woke up, returning her mother's chirp with a bright hum. Kessa gestured to Rivlion to join her and snuggled up against Nivyra. Milao jumped onto her mother's lap and chirped Kessa's name-sound brightly and quickly, and acknowledging her aunts with their own name-sounds. Soon, both mother and cat-child fell asleep. Lia sat beside her niece and sister, adjusting her glow until she, too, fell asleep, humming a stabilizing tune that drifted off as

Sera was talking with Rose about some diplomacy issue or another; Iridos was along for the ride, providing quips and comments from his perspective as the Feydorian king.
