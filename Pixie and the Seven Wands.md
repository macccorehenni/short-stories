## Into the Vale 
Pixie wandered into the field where her mother typically sat talking with the moths, the fruits of her power trailing behind her. 
"Glowcat?" she called out, allowing her power to strengthen, leaving even more, larger flowers behind. 
Pixie smiled as she saw her mother's glow. "Boo!" Pixie snuck up behind her, dropping focus on her power so she wouldn't spook Curiosity or Kindness, who Curiosity was talking to, oblivious to her surroundings. 
As Pixie approached, Curiosity jumped, causing her moth friend to flutter away, returning a moment later as Curiosity realized it was her preschooler. 
"'Sup, little one?" Curiosity smiled and pulled Pixie closer. "How was your first day?"
Pixie smiled. "I made two friends!"
"Oh, did you now?"
Pixie described her friends to her mother. One, a human named Volina, had a relatively unique manifest where she could generate static electricity just by raising her hand. She came from a family who was close to the Elders in terms of wealth, and has connection to them through Celestial Eisnore and its high school. She had come to her first day wearing a purple dress and shirt combo that Pixie admired, growing up around Curiosity and her high fashion sense.
She then went on to describe her other friend, Florynth. She was a Fey faerie, five inches tall, and had yellow and brown wings. She was wearing a pink and purple dress, and had on a orchid headdress belying her manifest as being the ability to shoot vines like Spiderman from her wrists. The three girls, much like Pixie's younger mother, seemed to be isolated due to the only ones with magic, Pixie having both mageics (thanks to her aunt Tot) and her Fey manifest on her side. But, they laughed over recess and lunch, even making each other friendship bracelets in crafts. 

"Other than meeting and hanging out with Vol and Flor, it was… boring."
"That's good. I didn't make any friends here when I came here, and you, little one? Two in your first day! I'm proud of you, little kit."
Emrie beamed, taking the compliment and spinning on her feet to hug Aven, who recoiled in fear for a minute, but huffed when he realized who had attacked him. 
Through bonds Pixie told her mother's dragon about the day. As Curiosity and Aven, snuggled closer, and both fell asleep. Ensuring she had properly bored her audience, she was bored herself now. So she moved on to find Esme, but she was not there. So she wandered towards the canyon lip. 
"So… cool! I no longer have to listen to *anyone*! 
But suddenly, something tripped the cat's feet. She momentarily thought it was Aven— he was nearby, his feet felt like this, he was large enough to cast a shadow of this size on Pixie— but something felt… off. 

She kept falling for what to her felt forever. Pixie had long since began panicking when she felt a sudden *thud* in her bones, followed a few seconds later by a dull, general pain across her body, as if she had followed. Pixie tried to sit up, but couldn't. Her arm had broken, she gravely reasoned. 

Suddenly, a loud voice broke her somewhat shock–induced stupor and, for whatever reason, set her street feline sense afire. It was an unfamiliar sound, yet one that somehow made sense. Pixie tried to make sense of it, but all she could hear was "wands". 
Suddenly clarity hit, and she realized _who_ the voice reminded her of, and she, despite her instincts fighting her tooth and nail, relaxed and focused on the voice. She noted its tone, something she was taught by the other street cats in her clique to identify _who_, exactly, a voice was. But she received the same confusing information from her non-addled brain— the voice was both her mothers at once? It had her younger mother's pitch, yet it had her older mother's timbre and intonation?

"Pixie?"

"Great, this voice knows my name too." She tucked her tail behind her less–injured arm, ready to spring up if needed. But she froze— this figure looked familiar, almost like her mothers— it had Dragonhair's spiky web of hair, and Glowcat's glowing skin. What was going on? She tensed her tail up more as the figure sheepishly approached her. 
"Don't touch me, evil guy!" Pixie was fully in protection mode, but her skills were a bit rusty. She tried to leap up, but her legs painfully crumpled behind her. When she tried again, she lost consciousness. 

---

Pixie woke up an hour later. 
"Ah, she has awoken now!"
The voice brought back some panic, but she noticed— she felt better! 
"Who are you? And why am I… *here*? Wherever here is…"
"I'll answer the second question first. Apologies for the way you found it… perhaps… your mothers won't punish you?"
"Who are you, again?"
"Ah, yes. My name is Iridia. I tend to forget to keep our leaders safe, my apologies… that's how the Wands found the last two unworthy… let's not worry about that."
"The Wands?" Pixie was interested in those two words. Her Aunt Tot hadn't judged her ready for a wand yet, but she realized that she— through some divine process— was going to become the wielder of these… Wands. 
"What are these Wands?"
"There are seven—
"The first made of juniper, its power to hurt and poison the bodies and minds of its enemies;
"The second of rowan, able to change the minds of Fate and its hems and its haws;
"The third of willow, able to cleanse or amplify emotions to suit the field of battle;
"The fourth of birch, taking advantage of time to turn its tide in one's favor;
"The fifth of sakura, overwhelming in its beauty and its strength;
"The sixth of baobab, able to plant walls of roots of barren lands; 
"And the seventh the most important of all, the sacred fig, a beauty indescribable."
